Privacy Policy for Brick Blast: Cannon Smash

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Brick Blast: Cannon Smash (Android package com.aliveappz.brickblast), a free mobile game published by AliveAppz ("we", "us"). By installing or playing the game you agree to this policy.

In short: the game has no accounts and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. The game shows adverts through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and advertising data to do that. Your game progress stays on your own device.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. Brick Blast: Cannon Smash does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location, and it has no sign-in. We do not run our own servers for the game and we receive no personal data from it.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only, so that it remembers where you left off:

  • the highest level you have unlocked and the stars earned on each level;
  • the cannon you have selected;
  • your sound and music settings.

This data never leaves your device through the game. It is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Brick Blast: Cannon Smash is supported by adverts supplied by Google AdMob. The game shows banner adverts on menu screens and optional rewarded video adverts, which you may choose to watch in exchange for extra shots or a bomb. To deliver and measure these adverts, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the game may automatically collect and process:

DataWhy it is used
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier provided by Android)Showing adverts, limiting how often you see the same advert, measuring advert performance and, where permitted, personalising adverts
IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) may be derivedChoosing relevant adverts, fraud prevention and security
Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type)Displaying adverts correctly on your device
Advert interactions (views, taps, whether a rewarded video was completed) and app diagnosticsMeasuring adverts, granting rewards and fixing technical problems

This data is collected and controlled by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your advertising choices

  • Consent in Europe and similar regions. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before adverts are requested. You can refuse personalised adverts and still play.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Turn off personalised adverts. You can manage ad personalisation for your Google account at adssettings.google.com.
  • Rewarded videos are optional. You are never required to watch a video to keep playing; you can retry the level at any time.

5. Permissions the game uses

  • Internet and network state – to load adverts.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Vibration – for short vibration feedback when shots land. No data is collected through it.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

6. Children

Brick Blast: Cannon Smash is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell personal information, and we have none to share. The only third party that receives data through the game is Google, as the advertising provider described in section 3.

8. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove the data stored on your device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. For data held by Google, use the controls described in section 4 or Google's own tools.

9. Security

Game progress is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Advert requests are sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example if new features, purchases or advert formats are added. The effective date at the top of this page will show when it was last revised. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
