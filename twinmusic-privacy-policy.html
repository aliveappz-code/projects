Twin Music Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · Applies to the Twin Music Android app (com.aliveappz.twinmusic)

The short version
  • Twin Music plays music files that are already on your phone. Your songs never leave your device.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no purchases.
  • Your settings and favourites are saved only on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data, such as the advertising ID, to show and measure ads.

1Who we are

Twin Music ("the app", "we", "us") is a music player that plays two songs at once, one in each ear. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

2Information that stays on your device

WhatWhyWhere it is kept
Your audio files (titles, artists, albums, folder names, duration)To list your songs and play themRead from your phone's media library. Never uploaded or shared.
Favourites, theme, progress style and flip settingTo remember your preferencesApp storage on your phone
Last played songs and positionsTo resume where you left offApp storage on your phone
Equalizer, speed, sleep timer and balance settingsTo apply your sound settingsApp memory and storage on your phone

We do not run servers for Twin Music, and we do not receive any of this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

3Advertising

Twin Music is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, including banner ads, full-screen ads, app-open ads and optional rewarded ads that unlock the Equalizer and Playlist for a short time. To deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location
  • Device and app information, such as model, OS version, language and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic and crash data related to ads

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings, under Settings › Privacy › Ads (the path may differ by device). Where required by law, for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy & ads.

5What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not use your microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We do not upload, sell or share your music or listening history.

6Children

Twin Music is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7Security and retention

Information on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox and stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Google retains ad data according to its own policies.

8Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
