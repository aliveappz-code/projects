Fruit Merger – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

The short version

  • Fruit Merger does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Your best score, saved game and settings stay on your device. We have no servers that store them.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device information to serve and measure ads. Details are below.
  • Fruit Merger is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13.

1. Who we are

Fruit Merger (the "App") is a casual puzzle game published by AliveAppz ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App uses, why, and what choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at aliveappz@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require registration or sign-in. The App does not collect or ask for:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address,
  • your contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone or camera,
  • your precise (GPS) location,
  • payment information. The App has no in-app purchases.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you stopped, the App saves the following locally on your device only:

  • your best score and the state of an unfinished game,
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, effects),
  • your daily-reward progress (last claim date, streak, bonus fruits) and whether you finished the tutorial.

This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show rewarded ads, interstitial ads and app-open ads. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process:

  • your Android advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location that can be derived from it,
  • device and software details (such as model, operating system version, language, network type),
  • information about how ads were shown and interacted with, and diagnostic data about the ad SDK.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the service secure. Depending on your settings and region, ads may be personalised. We do not receive your advertising ID or any personal profile from Google.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and Firebase data disclosure

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and similar regions): where the law requires it, the App shows a consent message (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads. You can change your choice when the App offers it, or by clearing the App's data to see the message again.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (or the equivalent on your device).

6. Sharing your score picture

The App can create a picture of your glass box and score. When you tap Share, the picture is passed to Android's standard share sheet and you choose the app or person to send it to. We do not receive or store the picture.

7. Children's privacy

Fruit Merger is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Data retention and your rights

Because we do not run a user database, we hold no personal information about you that we could access, correct or delete. Information stored by the App is on your device, and you can remove it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. Data processed by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies, and you can manage it using the choices in section 4.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Please contact us if you would like to exercise any right, and we will point you to the right place, including Google's own tools where Google is the data controller.

9. International transfers and security

Ad data processed by Google may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, under Google's safeguards. The App uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections where available. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the App.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add a feature or change an ad provider. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's store listing.
