1. Who we are

Status Vault (the "app") is an Android app that helps you keep statuses you have viewed, organize them in a private vault, and use tools such as a sticker maker, fancy text, direct chat links, QR codes, translation, and text statuses. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. What we do not collect

We do not operate a user database or a backend for this app. Specifically, we do not collect, receive, or store:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any account information (the app has no accounts);
  • the statuses, photos, videos, stickers, captions, or text you view, save, create, translate, or scan;
  • your contacts, messages, or chats;
  • your precise location;
  • payment information (the app has no purchases).

The app does not include any analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.

3. What the app handles on your device

The features below work with your content locally. This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Statuses and folder access

To show the statuses you have viewed, the app asks you to pick a folder (such as the WhatsApp ".Statuses" folder) using Android's built-in folder picker. The app can read only the folder you choose, and only because you granted access. You can change or remove this access at any time in the app's Privacy screen or in your Android settings.

Saved items, vault, and private folder

When you save a status, a copy is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Depending on your setting "Hide saves from gallery", a copy may also be added to your phone's gallery in a "StatusVault" folder. Items you move to the Private folder are protected by your fingerprint or screen lock, if you turn that on. Favorites and settings are stored locally on your device.

Stickers

Stickers and packs you create are stored in the app's private storage. When you tap "Add to WhatsApp", WhatsApp reads the selected pack from the app through Android's standard sticker mechanism. WhatsApp handles that content under its own privacy policy.

Fancy Text, Direct Chat, QR Code, Translate, Text Status

  • Fancy Text and Text Status run entirely on your device.
  • Direct Chat opens a link of the form wa.me/<number> in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business. The phone number and message you type are passed to that app on your device. We do not receive them.
  • QR Code creation runs on your device. Scanning uses Google Play services' code scanner, which shows the camera preview and returns the result to the app without the app needing camera permission.
  • Translate uses Google's ML Kit on-device translation. Your text is translated on your device and is not sent to a server. The first time you use a language, the app downloads a language model (roughly 30 MB) from Google.

Cut-out (background removal)

The sticker studio's "Magic cut" uses Google's ML Kit subject segmentation delivered through Google Play services. The image is processed on your device. The first use may download a small model from Google.

Reminders

If you turn on expiry reminders, the app periodically checks locally, in the background, whether any viewed status is close to expiring and shows a notification. No data is sent anywhere for this.

6. Your advertising choices

  • Consent form. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice later: when applicable, the Privacy screen shows a Privacy and ad choices option.
  • Android ad settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by device).
  • Go ad-free for a while. In the Privacy screen you can watch one short video to turn off banner and full-screen ads for one hour. Watching a video is always your choice.

7. When the app uses the internet

The app works offline for most features. It uses the internet to load ads, to show the consent form, and to download translation or cut-out models. It does not send your statuses, stickers, or text to any server operated by us.

8. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone, except that the advertising and Google services described in section 5 receive the information needed to function.

When you share something using the app (for example, you share a saved status, a sticker, or a QR code through Android's share sheet or to WhatsApp), it is sent to the app you choose, under that app's rules. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, although because we hold no user data, we expect there is nothing to disclose.

9. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Where it lives. Everything you save is stored on your device, not with us.
  • Delete from inside the app. Open the Privacy screen and tap Delete everything to remove your vault and Private folder. You can also delete single items, remove stickers or packs, or turn on Auto-delete after 30 days (favorites are kept).
  • Gallery copies. If a copy was added to your phone's gallery, delete it there too. It is stored outside the app's private storage.
  • Uninstalling. Removing the app deletes its private storage, including the vault, Private folder, sticker packs, and settings.
  • Ad data. Information held by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies and controlled through your Google and Android settings.

Because there are no accounts and we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

10. Security

Your saved items are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can protect the Private folder with your fingerprint, face, or device lock. Data sent to ad services travels over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please also keep your device updated and locked.
