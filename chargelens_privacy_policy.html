1. What ChargeLens does

ChargeLens reads the battery information that Android makes available to apps and shows it to you: how fast your phone is charging, how long it may take to fill, battery temperature, and how your charging habits look over time. It can compare cables and chargers, record charging sessions, show an optional full-screen charging display, send charge alerts, and (if you allow it) estimate which apps use the most battery.

3. Permissions and why the app asks for them

Android shows some of these permissions to you directly. Features that need a permission simply stay off until you allow it.

Features that use your phone but need no permission

  • Flashlight button on the charging display turns your phone's torch on and off. ChargeLens does not take photos or video, and cannot see what the camera sees. The camera button only opens your phone's own camera app.
  • Spoken alerts (optional, off by default) use your phone's built-in text-to-speech to read a short battery message aloud. The message is created on your phone.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ChargeLens is free and supported by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show, measure and protect against fraud in ads, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • your advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and the approximate location that can be worked out from it;
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, app name and version, language);
  • how you interact with ads (shown, tapped, closed).

This information goes straight from the Google SDK to Google. We do not receive it and we cannot see it. Google's use of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your advertising choices

  • On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path can vary by phone) to opt out of ads personalisation or to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • You can also manage ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • In regions where consent applies, use Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app to review or withdraw your consent.
  • Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less tailored to you.

Where ads appear

  • Banner ads at the bottom of the main screens (Meter, Cables, Diagnose, History and Smart hibernate).
  • Occasional full-screen ads at natural pauses, such as after a cable test, when you leave a charge replay, or when you leave Smart hibernate after stopping an app. They are limited to a few per session, never at app start and never right after you change a setting.
  • Optional rewarded videos: you can choose to watch one to unlock the Tornado and Heart charging screens for 24 hours. This is always your choice.
  • We never show ads on your lock screen, in the charging display or in notifications.

Your consent (EEA, UK and similar regions)

Where the law requires it, ChargeLens asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can choose to allow or limit personalised ads. If you decline, ads may still appear but will not be personalised. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in the app's Settings (shown where consent applies).

5. Sharing and selling

  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising purposes ourselves.
  • The only third party that receives information because you use the app is Google, through the AdMob SDK described above.
  • If you tap Share ranking, the text of your cable ranking is passed to the app you pick. We do not see what you do with it.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it. Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is normally nothing to disclose.

6. Storage, backup and deletion

  • Where it lives: history, cable tests and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them.
  • Backup: if you have turned on Google's Android backup for your phone, Android may include this app's data in your backup and restore it on a new phone. That is controlled by your phone and Google account settings, not by us.
  • How long we keep it: the app keeps your most recent charging sessions (up to 60) and cable tests (up to 40). Older ones are replaced automatically.
  • Delete it yourself: in the app, go to History → Settings → Your data and tap Clear charging history or Clear cable tests. Uninstalling the app, or choosing Clear storage in Android's app settings, removes everything the app stored.

7. Security

Because your battery information stays on your device and is not sent to us, the main protection is Android's own app sandbox, which keeps other apps from reading it. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we keep the amount of information the app handles small on purpose. Information sent to Google for ads is protected by Google's security measures.

8. Children

ChargeLens is a general-audience utility app and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given information to us, contact us and we will help. Parents can limit ad personalisation for a child's device in Google's family and ad settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California and other regions), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information, to object to certain uses, and to withdraw consent.

  • For information the app keeps on your phone, you already have full control: view it in the app, delete it as described in section 6, and withdraw any permission in Android settings.

  • We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

10. International processing
