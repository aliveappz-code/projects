🐶 Coco: Talking Puppy

Privacy Policy · AliveAppz

Effective date: 28 November 2026

In short: Coco: Talking Puppy does not ask for your name, does not have accounts and does not record or upload your voice. The app shows family-friendly, non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, which collect limited device information to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

Who we are

Coco: Talking Puppy (package com.aliveappz.cocopuppy) is made by AliveAppz (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or location.
  • No sign-up or account of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases or payment information.

Microphone

Coco can repeat what you say. When the microphone is switched on (with your permission), sound is processed only on your device to make Coco's puppy voice and is discarded straight after playback. Voice is never recorded to storage, never uploaded and never shared. You can switch the microphone off at any time with the mic button, or turn off the permission in your device settings.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device only: Coco's look, how hungry or sleepy he is, your settings and level. This data never leaves the device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Because the app is intended for children:

  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, so ads are not personalised and are not based on interests or past behaviour.
  • Ads are limited to a G (general audiences) content rating.
  • The app does not use the Android Advertising ID.
  • Full-screen ads only appear at natural breaks in play, never when the app opens. Optional “watch an ad” rewards are always chosen by the player.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, app set ID, IP address (used to estimate a general region) and ad interactions. Google processes this under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and Families ads requirements.

Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, a Google consent message may be shown. A grown-up can change these choices any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the app.

Children's privacy

We designed Coco: Talking Puppy for children and follow Google Play's Families Policy and applicable laws such as COPPA and GDPR-K. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data security

Game data stays on your device. Information handled by Google's ad SDK is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • Turn the microphone on or off with the in-app mic button or device settings.
  • Change ad consent choices in Settings → Privacy choices (where available).
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data stored on the device.
