Privacy policy

Your voice, your choices, and how the app handles information.

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Orbit Robot (“Orbit,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use our Android talking robot app.

It covers talking and voice replay, dancing, songs, laughter, virtual charging, and advertising. Our privacy and support contact is aliveappz@gmail.com. The Android package is com.orbitstudio.robot.

At a glance: Voice recordings are processed on your device and are not uploaded by Orbit. No account is required. The app stores a few preferences locally. Google AdMob processes certain device and usage information to provide ads.

1. Microphone and voice playback

Orbit requests Android microphone permission to listen to short phrases and repeat the recording with a lightweight robot audio effect. The microphone feature is enabled by default once permission is granted and the Home screen is ready. It repeatedly listens and plays back phrases while active.

Audio is processed locally in temporary memory. Orbit does not upload your recordings to us, Google AdMob, or a speech service, save them as audio files, or use them to create voice profiles. The app does not transcribe speech or run speech-recognition or voice-generation models. Audio remains in temporary memory and is not saved or uploaded.

Capture buffers are released when listening stops. The most recent replay clip can remain in memory until it is replaced or the app session ends. Listening pauses during playback, Settings, fullscreen ads, consent forms, and when the app is in the background.

You can turn listening off with the microphone icon or revoke microphone permission in Android Settings. Dancing, songs, and laughter remain available without microphone permission.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores sound and gentle-motion preferences, the robot’s virtual battery level, and an age category on your device so these choices can be remembered.

Before advertising starts, Orbit asks for a birth year to select age-appropriate advertising settings. The entered year is used temporarily to determine a category and is not saved or sent to Google. Only the category—Younger, Adult, or Unknown—is stored locally. Choosing “Prefer not to say” uses the more protective Unknown category.

Google’s consent tools may also store privacy choices and consent status on your device. Orbit does not require a name, account, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Orbit uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Ad-supported versions may display banners, interstitial ads between activities, and optional rewarded ads.

Google’s advertising SDK processes information such as your IP address (which can indicate general location), app and ad interactions, performance diagnostics, and device or app identifiers. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Availability of identifiers depends on the device and privacy settings. For adult users, Google may access the Android advertising ID where available, subject to device settings and applicable consent. Younger and unknown-age users receive child-directed ad treatment configured before the ad SDK initializes. Orbit requests non-personalized ads for all users; these settings do not eliminate all advertising data processing.

Orbit does not supply microphone audio or the entered birth year to the advertising SDK. We do not include a separate app analytics service. Where applicable, privacy messages are presented before ads are requested, and Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you revisit available choices.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads’ data disclosures. Advertiser websites or apps you choose to open have their own privacy policies.

4. Children and family use

Orbit is intended for children and adults. Younger and unknown-age users receive child-directed advertising treatment and banners only; interstitial and rewarded ads are disabled for those categories. The app requests Google’s G ad-content rating for all users and non-personalized ads. Because only a birth year is entered, users in their 18th birth year are also treated as Younger.

Children’s voice playback stays on the device as described above. Orbit has no public profiles, messaging between users, or uploads of recordings. Parents and guardians can disable microphone access and clear local app data. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can review it and arrange deletion where appropriate. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support for them.

5. Support messages and sharing

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot issues, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending voice recordings or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider. Advertising information is processed by Google as described above. We may disclose information we hold when required by law or when necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or protect legal rights. We do not sell microphone recordings or support correspondence.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Voice audio: temporary device memory only, as described in section 1; there is no server-side voice archive to delete.
  • Local preferences and age category: remain until you clear the app’s storage or remove the app, subject to Android backup or restore behavior. Clearing storage resets the app and its saved choices.
  • Support correspondence: retained as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal or security obligations. You can request deletion by email.
  • Google advertising and consent information: retained under Google’s retention policies. Clearing Orbit’s storage does not delete information already processed by Google.

No Orbit account is created, so there is no account-deletion step. For requests about information held by us, email aliveappz@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify and locate your request.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can control microphone permission in Android Settings, switch the in-app mic off, clear local storage, and use the available ad privacy choices. Optional rewarded ads are not required for normal charging.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information, object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to make a request about data we hold. Google’s linked policies explain controls for data it processes.

8. Security and international processing

We limit Orbit’s voice processing to the device. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No device, transmission method, or storage system is completely secure.

Google and our email provider may process information on servers outside your country. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards and practices that apply to their services.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will publish the revised policy here and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before new processing begins.
