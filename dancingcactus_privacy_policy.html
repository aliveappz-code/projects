Dancing Cactus: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

In short: Dancing Cactus is a toy app for children. It listens through the microphone so the cactus can repeat what is said, and that sound never leaves the device. The app has no accounts and asks for no names, emails or photos. It shows adverts from Google AdMob, set up for children: no personalised ads and no advertising ID.

Information the app uses on the device

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Microphone sound So the cactus can hear when someone speaks and repeat it back in a funny voice. It stays on the device. Sound is held in a temporary file only long enough to be played back, and is replaced by the next recording. It is never uploaded, shared, or used for advertising. The microphone is switched off when the app is paused or in the background.
Style choices (hat, accessories, pot colour, lights, backdrop, voice) So the cactus looks the same the next time the app is opened. Saved on the device only. Removed when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, precise location, or any account details. The app has no sign-in, no chat, no social features and no in-app purchases.

Advertising

The app is free and shows full-screen adverts provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Adverts appear at natural pauses, such as after choosing a song or finishing dressing the cactus.

Because the app is made for children, we have configured AdMob as follows:

  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children, so Google serves non-personalised adverts only.
  • Adverts are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences ("G").
  • The app does not request the Android advertising ID.

To deliver and measure adverts and to prevent fraud, Google's advertising software automatically receives some technical information from the device, such as its IP address (which indicates an approximate location such as country or city), device type, operating system version, app version, and information about which adverts were shown or tapped. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies your child. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Other internet use

The first time the app is opened it downloads its display font from Google Fonts. Like any internet request, this shares the device's IP address with Google. No other information is sent.

Children's privacy

Dancing Cactus is designed for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We aim to comply with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation as they apply to children, and the Google Play Families Policy.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe the app has collected personal information from your child, please contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and we will help.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: to hear speech so the cactus can repeat it. Without it, the songs, dressing up and touch play still work.
  • Internet: to load adverts and the display font.

Keeping and deleting data

We do not keep any data about you or your child on our own servers, because we do not collect any. Everything the app stores is on the device and can be removed by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Your choices

  • You can turn off the microphone permission at any time in Android settings.
  • You can pause listening at any time with the large button in the app.
  • You can stop adverts from loading by using the app without an internet connection.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how children's information is handled, we will also say so in the app's store listing.
