Privacy Policy

Tidewrite: Sand Art · by AliveAppz · Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Tidewrite does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email or location. Your drawings, photos and videos stay on your phone. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to do that. Details are below.

1. Who we are

Tidewrite: Sand Art ("the app", "Tidewrite") is made by AliveAppz ("we", "us"). You can reach us at aliveappz@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

These things are created and stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We do not upload them and we cannot see them.

  • Photos you keep from your drawings, and timelapse videos you make
  • Your settings, such as tide speed, sound, haptics, time of day, and your last shore
  • The last word you wrote with the Name tool, and your drawing streak
  • A one-day Pro pass, if you unlock one by watching an ad

The drawing itself lives in memory while you draw. It is not saved unless you choose to keep it.

3. Photos you choose

If you use the "From my photo" trace guide, the app opens Android's photo picker and you choose one picture. The app turns that picture into faint outlines on your phone. It is not uploaded, and the app does not get access to the rest of your photos.

4. Sharing

When you press Share, the app hands your picture or video to Android's share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you pick, for example a messaging app. We do not receive it.

5. Ads and the information they use

Tidewrite is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. We show banner ads on the Shores and Memories pages, occasional full-screen ads when you share, and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch to unlock the timelapse or a Pro shore for a day. We do not show ads while you are drawing.

To show and measure ads, Google's ads software may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • Device and app information, and how you interact with ads
  • Diagnostics such as crash and performance data

We ask for ads to be suitable for a general audience (a PG maximum rating). We do not give Google or anyone else your drawings, photos or videos. Google's use of information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent form. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other places, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load. You can change your choice later in the app under Tide & feel, "Ad privacy choices", where it is available.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's Settings, under Privacy, Ads.

6. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number or password
  • No precise location. The app does not ask for location permission
  • No access to your contacts, microphone, camera or files
  • No sale of personal information

8. Children

Tidewrite is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, write to aliveappz@gmail.com and we will help.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your drawings, photos and settings stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete a saved picture or video in the app under Memories. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything the app has stored. Because we hold no account or server data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. For data held by Google for ads, see the links in section 5.

10. Security

Your files are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified phone. Ads are loaded over secure connections.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights over personal data, such as access, correction, deletion and objecting to certain uses. Since we do not hold personal data about you, these mostly apply to Google's ad data. You can use the choices in section 5 or contact us at aliveappz@gmail.com and we will point you in the right direction.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
